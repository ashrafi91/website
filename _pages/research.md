# Research

My research centers on biomedical health informatics. I build deep learning methods for electronic health records and medical images, and my broader interests include bioinformatics, computer vision, machine learning, recommendation techniques, optimization, and human&ndash;computer interaction.
See the [publications]({{ '/publications' | relative_url }}) page for the resulting papers.

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Biomedical Health Informatics</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Deep learning on electronic health records (MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV). I developed a multi-modal, multi-label network for automated ICD-9 coding of rare disease admissions (MMAC-Net, <em>Applied Sciences</em>, 2026); in the course project behind it (CPSC 671) the network reached a Micro-AUC of 0.997 and a Precision@8 of 0.875. I am now building a multi-expert agentic framework for disease diagnosis from EHR data (CPSC 559), using LLM-based diagnostic agents such as BioMedNLP, Mistral-7B, Phi-2, and Zephyr-7B.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Autism Spectrum Disorder Classification</h2>
<p class="research-desc">M.Sc. dissertation at the Islamic University of Technology (2024&ndash;2025). I developed graph-based neural networks that use multi-modal data to separate autism spectrum disorder (ASD) from typical development, including an enhanced graph convolutional network with Chebyshev spectral graphs and graph attention (IVCNZ 2025), alongside real-time data collection to validate the findings.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Medical Image Analysis</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Attention-based and transformer-based models for medical images: DoubleU-NetPlus for semantic segmentation (<em>Neural Computing and Applications</em>, 2023), attention-based transfer learning for bone fracture classification from musculoskeletal radiographs (ICCIT 2024), and SwinMedNet for diabetic retinopathy classification on RetinaMNIST2D (ICEEICT 2024).</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Computer Vision</h2>
<p class="research-desc">BFERNet, a few-shot facial expression recognition model for babies built on ResNet12 with convolutional block attention modules (<em>IEEE Access</em>, 2025); chemical laboratory object detection with YOLOv8 and YOLOv9 (ICCIT 2024); and MCFFA-Net for apple foliar disease classification (ICCIT 2022).</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Bioinformatics</h2>
<p class="research-desc">An algorithm for DNA motif finding and ranking that handles variable-length motifs and mutation, built on hash tables and integer mapping (B.Sc. dissertation; ReTIS 2015); gene regulatory network prediction from expression profiles (ICCIT 2016); and genetic sequence compression with machine learning and arithmetic coding (ICCIT 2022). I also supervised undergraduate theses on compressing DNA and RNA with deep learning (8% lower bits-per-base than state-of-the-art work) and with pattern indexing (about 58% compression).</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Machine Learning, Recommendation and Optimization</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Applied machine learning on text and behavioral data: mapping personality traits to perceived stress in undergraduates (<em>IJMECS</em>, 2019) and detecting character assassination through troll comments on social media with psycholinguistic tools (ECCE 2019). On the optimization side, clustering-based Max-Min scheduling in cloud environments (<em>IJACSA</em>, 2017). I also supervised undergraduate projects on data scraping with personalized recommendation for e-commerce and news portals, movie recommendation, and credit card fraud detection.</p>
</div>
